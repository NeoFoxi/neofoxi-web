Privacy

Controller: NeoFoxi, m@neofoxi.net.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes connection data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver and secure the site. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; transfer under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

The site stores two first-party cookies, both for one year:

  • lang — your language. Set only when you select a non-English language.
  • theme — light or dark mode. Set when you toggle the theme.

Both are technically necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2)(2) TDDDG), so no consent banner is needed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can delete them in your browser at any time.

GitHub statistics

The statistics section loads numbers directly from api.github.com, transmitting your IP address and user agent to GitHub Inc. (USA). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; transfer under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. No cookies are set.

Fonts and icons

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare). When the page loads, your IP address and user agent are transmitted to these providers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; transfers are based on the providers' EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification or standard contractual clauses.

Contact

If you email me, your address and message are processed to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, and to object to processing. You may also complain to a supervisory authority. Contact: m@neofoxi.net.
